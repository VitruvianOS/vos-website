0.3.1 is out. Thanks to everyone for the support following the first public release.

What's new in this ISO:

* Most icon problems are fixed
* New apps and preferences
* Double buffering in app_server
* Fixed boot on BIOS machines
* General stability improvements
* Tracker fixes
* Kernel fixes

What happened behind the scenes (not shipped yet):

* ARM port progress
* Filesystem improvements

A few things to keep in mind:

* Running on real hardware isn't recommended at this point unless you specifically want to. A VM is fine for trying things out.
* Apps can crash, the system can freeze, threads can spin. This is alpha software.
* The filesystem still needs work and will be the focus of the next release.

Thanks again to everyone who supported us on [DistroWatch](https://v-os.dev/news/support-us-on-distrowatch/) — top 10!
